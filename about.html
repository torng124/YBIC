ABOUT YBIC

Who We Are

YBIC – Youth Bright Impact Club is a youth volunteer initiative focused on education, community service and creating brighter opportunities for children.

OUR IDENTITY

Young People. One Purpose.

YBIC connects young volunteers who believe that education can open doors and create opportunities.

  • Volunteer-led organization
  • English education
  • Creative learning
  • Youth leadership
  • Community service
MISSION & VISION

What Guides Our Work

🎯

Our Mission

To support children with accessible and engaging English learning while giving young volunteers meaningful opportunities to serve.

🌟

Our Vision

A future where every child has the confidence to learn, communicate and imagine a brighter future.

❤️

Our Values

Respect, teamwork, responsibility, creativity and care for our community.
